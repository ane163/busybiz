\# BusyBiz — Business Management SaaS \& Marketplace



BusyBiz is a modern business management SaaS and marketplace platform designed to help small businesses manage their day-to-day operations from one centralized system.



The platform combines business management tools with a marketplace where businesses can showcase their products and customers can discover products and contact sellers.



\## 🚀 Overview



BusyBiz provides businesses with a centralized platform for managing products, customers, orders, inventory, suppliers, invoices, receipts, expenses, reports, and business insights.



The system also includes a marketplace component that allows businesses to advertise their products and customers to browse available listings.



The application is designed with a professional \*\*black, white, and gold\*\* interface and focuses on usability, organization, and scalability.
